## 1. First-Time Launch & App Tour

When you open GharKharcha for the first time:
- **Interactive Helper Wizard:** An interactive 5-step tour introduces the app's core concepts:
  1. *Track Every Rupee:* Centralized dashboard for construction costs.
  2. *Works & Budgets:* Trade categories and budget monitoring.
  3. *Daily Attendance & Labour:* One-tap attendance and wage logs.
  4. *Materials, Shops & Bills:* Bill receipt photo scanner and shop credit.
  5. *Reports & Safety:* PDF statements, biometric security, and full ZIP backup.
- **Revisit the Tour Anytime:** Go to the **More** tab and tap **App tour & user guide**.
- **Try Demo Data:** If you want to explore the app with ready-made data:
  1. Open the **More** tab.
  2. Tap **Settings**.
  3. Scroll to **Demo Data** at the bottom and tap **Load sample data**.
  4. The app will immediately populate a sample house (*Dream Villa*), 3 works, 4 labourers, materials, and 2 weeks of attendance.

## 2. Managing Houses (Projects)

You can manage multiple houses, plots, or renovation works simultaneously.

### Adding a House:
1. On the **Home** tab, tap **Add house** (or go to **More** -> **My houses** -> **+**).
2. Enter:
   - **House name:** e.g., *Dream Villa*, *Plot 42*, or *First Floor Renovation*.
   - **Address:** e.g., *Sector 62, Noida*.
   - **Start date:** The day construction began.
   - **Total budget:** The estimated cost (e.g., ₹25,00,000).
   - **Cover photo:** Tap the camera icon to photograph the plot or house.
3. Tap **Save**.

### Switching Between Houses:
- Tap the **House Name** at the top of the Home or Works screen to open the house switcher dropdown. Tap any house to make it active.

## 3. Works & Trade Budgets

Houses are organized by distinct construction trades ("Works").

### Common Work Types:
- **Brick Masonry & Plaster** (ईंट चिनाई और प्लास्टर)
- **Electrical** (बिजली वायरिंग)
- **Plumbing & Sanitary** (नल-फिटिंग)
- **Painting** (पेंटिंग)
- **Tiling** (टाइल्स)
- **Carpentry** (बढ़ई का काम)
- **Custom / Other** (अन्य कार्य)

### Adding a Work:
1. Go to the **Works** tab and tap the **+** button.
2. Select the work type or type a custom name.
3. Enter the allocated budget for this specific trade (e.g., ₹3,50,000 for Masonry).
4. Set status: *Planned*, *In Progress*, *On Hold*, or *Done*.
5. Tap **Save**.

### Budget Alerts:
- If expenses reach **80%** of the work budget, a yellow warning banner appears on the dashboard.
- If expenses exceed **100%**, a red **Over budget** alert appears showing the exact overspend amount.

## 4. Labourers Directory & One-Tap Attendance

### Adding Labourers:
1. Go to the **Labour** tab and tap **Add labourer**.
2. Enter:
   - **Name:** e.g., *Ramesh Kumar*.
   - **Skill:** *Brick mason*, *Helper*, *Electrician*, *Plumber*, *Carpenter*, *Painter*, *Tile fitter*, *Welder*, or *Other*.
   - **Phone number:** For quick calls and WhatsApp messages.
   - **Standard Daily Wage:** e.g., ₹700/day.
   - **Hourly Rate:** Automatically calculated (Daily Wage ÷ standard hours), or enter a custom rate.
   - **Profile photo:** Optional photo for easy recognition.
3. Tap **Save**.

### Marking Daily Attendance:
1. Tap **Daily Attendance** on the Home screen or tap **Mark attendance** on the Labour tab.
2. Select the **Work** (e.g., *Brick Masonry*).
3. Tap each labourer's status button:
   - **Full:** Full workday (credited with their daily wage rate).
   - **Half:** Half-day (credited with 50% daily wage).
   - **Hours:** Enter custom hours worked.
   - **Absent:** Marks the worker absent.
4. **Overtime & Advances:** Tap the arrow next to any labourer to log overtime hours or daily cash advances.
5. Tap **Save**.

### Time-Saving Attendance Tools:
- **Copy Previous Day:** Tap **Copy previous day** to copy yesterday's attendance in one tap.
- **Repeat for Date Range:** If the same team worked for multiple days without changes, tap **Repeat this day for other dates** to log an entire week in seconds.

## 5. Labourer Ledgers, Advances & Wage Payments

### Viewing a Labourer's Running Ledger:
1. In the **Labour** tab, tap any labourer's card.
2. The screen shows:
   - **Days worked** and **total hours**.
   - **Earned:** Total wages accumulated from attendance.
   - **Paid:** Total money disbursed.
   - **Balance:** Color-coded net due (red) or advance (green).
   - A day-wise audit table listing every attendance entry and payment.

### Making a Payment:
1. Tap **Add payment** (or **Settle up**) in the labourer ledger.
2. Enter:
   - **Amount:** Pre-filled with the exact pending dues, or enter a custom amount.
   - **Payment type:** **Wage** (settles earned work) or **Advance** (money given before work).
   - **Payment mode:** **Cash**, **UPI**, **Bank Transfer**, or **Cheque**.
   - **Reference / UTR:** Transaction ID for UPI or Cheque number.
   - **Receipt photo:** Take a photo of the signed payment voucher or UPI screenshot.
3. Tap **Save**. The balance updates instantly.

## 6. Materials, Bills & Photo Capture

### Logging Material Purchases:
1. Open the target Work details screen, go to the **Materials** tab, and tap **+** (or use the **Quick Add** FAB on Home).
2. Choose entry mode:
   - **Item details:** Enter item name (e.g., *UltraTech Cement*), brand, quantity (e.g., *50*), unit (*bag*), and rate (*₹380*). The total calculates automatically.
   - **Total only:** Quick mode when you only have a consolidated bill amount (e.g., *Raw building material – ₹14,500*).
3. **Shop / Vendor:** Select an existing shop or type a new shop name.
4. **Payment status:**
   - Toggle **Paid** ON if you paid the shop in full.
   - Toggle **Paid** OFF if you bought on credit (khata/udhar). The app will track this under unpaid shop dues.
5. **Attach Bill Photo:** Tap **Add photo** -> **Take photo** to snap the paper bill.
6. Tap **Save**.

## 7. Vendors & Shop Credit Management

1. Open the **More** tab and tap **Shops & vendors**.

## 8. Dashboard, Charts & House Breakdown

### Home Dashboard:
- **Grand Total Spent:** Combined material and labour expenditure.
- **Today's Labour Cost:** Live metric showing today's attendance expense.
- **Pending Labour Dues:** Total wages owed across all workers.
- **Quick-Add Speed Dial:** Floating Action Button (FAB) on Home allowing one-tap addition of **Material**, **Attendance**, or **Payment**.

### Visual Charts:
- **Spend by Work Donut Chart:** Shows percentage distribution of funds across trades with interactive color keys.
- **30-Day Daily Spend Bar Chart:** Visual timeline of expenses over the past month. Tap any bar to see the date and exact amount.

### House Breakdown (5 Tabs):
Tap the **House breakdown** banner on Home to explore:
1. **By Work:** Spend vs. budget per trade.
2. **By Category:** Material vs. Labour percentage bar and unpaid shop total.
3. **By Month:** Month-on-month expense trends.
4. **By Labourer:** Individual earnings, days worked, and net balances.
5. **By Vendor:** Shop-wise purchase totals and credit balances.

## 10. Generating & Sharing PDF / CSV Reports

GharKharcha generates professional, printable documents using Android's native graphics engine (zero internet or cloud required).

### Steps to Generate a Report:
1. Open the **More** tab and tap **Reports & statements** (or tap **Export report** from any Work or Labourer ledger).
2. Choose **Report type**:
   - **House summary:** Complete overview of all works, category split, and monthly totals.
   - **Work report:** Itemized materials purchase register, attendance log, and optional bill photo appendices.
   - **Labourer statement:** Formal attendance log, payments ledger, and **Dual Signature Lines** (Labourer & Contractor).
3. Select **Format**:
   - **PDF document:** Standard A4 printable document with teal branding, zebra striping, and table pagination.
   - **CSV spreadsheet:** RFC 4180 format with UTF-8 BOM, ready to open in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
4. Choose **Date range**: *All time*, *This month*, *Last month*, or *Custom range*.
5. Tap **Generate & Share**.
6. The Android Share Sheet will appear: share instantly via **WhatsApp**, **Google Drive**, **Gmail**, or send directly to a **Printer**.

## 11. Settings, App Lock, Reminders & Backup

Open the **More** tab and tap **Settings**:

### Appearance:
- **Theme:** Choose between **System default**, **Light**, or **Dark** mode.
- **Dynamic colour:** Toggle wallpaper-matching colors (Android 12+).

### Work & Currency:
- **Default hours per day:** Use the `-` and `+` steppers to configure standard working hours (1 to 24 hrs, default 8 hrs).
- **Currency symbol:** Indian Rupee (`₹`).

### Security & App Lock:
- Enable **App lock** to require fingerprint, face unlock, or device PIN/pattern whenever the app is opened or resumed from the background.

### Reminders:
- **Daily attendance reminder:** Receive a notification at your chosen time (e.g., 7:00 PM) if today's attendance has not been recorded.
- **Weekly payday reminder:** Receive a weekend reminder showing total pending labour dues across all sites.

### Complete Backup & Restore (100% Offline):
- **Create backup:** Tap **Create backup** to save a single `.zip` file containing your entire SQLite database, all bill/photo attachments, and a `manifest.json`. You choose the folder via Android's Storage Access Framework (Google Drive, SD Card, Downloads).
- **Restore backup:** Tap **Restore backup**, select your previously saved `.zip` archive, and confirm. All data and photos are restored cleanly.

## 12. Switching Languages (English / हिन्दी)

GharKharcha supports full bilingual localization in English and Hindi.
- **System Language:** If your phone's system language is set to Hindi (हिन्दी), GharKharcha will automatically display in Hindi.
- **Per-App Language (Android 13+):**
  1. Open your phone's **Settings**.
  2. Go to **System** -> **Languages** -> **App Languages**.
  3. Select **GharKharcha** and choose **हिन्दी** or **English**.
